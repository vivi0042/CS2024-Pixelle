About Us

Founded in 2024 at a Tampines HDB study desk, Pixelle began with a bullet journal full of empty boxes labelled "photo here".

The photos were all on a phone. Printing them meant a kiosk queue, glue sticks and procrastination. Premium pocket printers cost S$150 or more, and budget ones printed faint black-and-white receipts that faded within months.

So we built the printer we wanted: full colour, every print a sticker, zero ink, and priced fairly with affordable refills. Our name says it all: pixels you can finally hold.

Mission

To get the best moments out of the camera roll and into people's hands, journals and homes, simply and affordably.

Vision

To be Singapore's favourite way to print memories, the first thing people reach for at birthdays, hall events and family visits.

Values

Honest specs, friendly local support, and creativity for everyone, whatever your budget.
